# APUSH — AP United States History

## Overview
> **Study plan:** [STUDY-PLAN.md](STUDY-PLAN.md) | **Resources:** [../RESOURCES.md](../RESOURCES.md) | **Grading:** [../GRADING.md](../GRADING.md)
AP US History from 1491 to present. Heavy on reading, writing (SAQ/DBQ/LEQ), and thematic analysis.

## Grading
Major 60% (tests, DBQs/LEQs, projects) / Minor 40% (HW, classwork, reading quizzes, SAQs)

## Class strategy
- **Pre-read the textbook section before class** — APUSH is reading-heavy; if you fall behind on reading, you can't catch up
- **Heimler's History** on YouTube — watch the matching video the night before each topic
- **Take Cornell-style notes** in class — questions on left, notes on right, summary at bottom
- **Practice thesis statements constantly** — DBQ and LEQ live or die on the thesis
- **Make a running timeline** all year — by each 6-week period you should have a growing timeline
- **SAQ practice** — short answer questions are where easy points live; practice the format
- **Anki:** Key terms, dates, amendments, court cases, presidents — cloze deletion works well
- **Biggest risk:** Falling behind on reading. One missed chapter compounds. Never skip the reading.

## Key resources
- Heimler's History (YouTube) — gold standard for APUSH
- AP Classroom AP Daily videos
- Gilder Lehrman timelines
- Princeton Review / 5 Steps to a 5 (for exam prep in spring)

## 6-week approach
- Each 6-week period covers ~2-3 historical periods
- Tests likely include MCQ + SAQ or DBQ
- DBQ grading is rubric-based — learn the rubric (thesis, contextualization, evidence, sourcing/HIPP, complexity)
