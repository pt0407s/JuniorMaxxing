# Free Online Resources for Every AP Course

All resources below are free unless marked otherwise. These are the best publicly available materials for each course — official College Board content, established YouTube educators, and community-built study sites.

---

## AP Biology

### Official (College Board)
- **AP Biology CED (Course & Exam Description)** — the master document. Contains all units, learning objectives, science practices, and sample exam questions. [PDF on AP Central](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap-biology-course-and-exam-description.pdf)
- **AP Classroom** — your teacher will give you a join code. Has Progress Checks (MCQ + FRQ) for every unit, AP Daily videos, and topic questions.
- **AP Biology Equations & Formulas Sheet** — provided on exam day, but study it now.
- **Past FRQs + Scoring Guidelines** — [AP Central exam page](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-biology/exam). Every FRQ from past years with official scoring. This is gold.
- **AP Biology Lab Manual** — required 25% of class is labs. Know the lab manual.

### Video Resources
- **Bozeman Science (Paul Andersen)** — [bozemanscience.com/review](http://www.bozemanscience.com/review) — the OG AP Bio resource. Unit-by-unit videos covering every topic. Old but still aligned to the big ideas.
- **AP Biology YouTube search** — search any topic + "AP Biology" and multiple teachers will appear.

### Study Sites
- **Fiveable AP Bio** — [fiveable.me/ap-bio](https://fiveable.me/ap-bio) — unit study guides, cheatsheets, key terms, practice questions. Free.
- **OpenStax Biology for AP Courses** — [openstax.org](https://openstax.org/books/biology-ap-courses) — free textbook aligned to AP Bio. Use as reference.

### Unit Weighting (where to focus)
| Unit | Weight |
|------|--------|
| Unit 1: Chemistry of Life | 8-11% |
| Unit 2: Cells | 10-13% |
| Unit 3: Cellular Energetics | 12-16% |
| Unit 4: Cell Communication & Cell Cycle | 10-15% |
| Unit 5: Heredity | 8-11% |
| Unit 6: Gene Expression & Regulation | 12-16% |
| Unit 7: Natural Selection | 13-20% (HIGHEST) |
| Unit 8: Ecology | 10-15% |

**Focus:** Unit 7 (Natural Selection) is the heaviest. Units 3 and 6 are also high-weight.

### Exam Format
- 60 MCQ (90 min) — 50% of score
- 6 FRQ: 2 long (9 pts each) + 4 short (4 pts each) (90 min) — 50% of score
- Calculator allowed on both sections

## APUSH (AP US History)

### Official (College Board)
- **APUSH CED** — on AP Central
- **AP Classroom** — Progress Checks, AP Daily videos
- **Past FRQs + Scoring Guidelines** — DBQs, LEQs, SAQs from every year

### Video Resources
- **Heimler's History** — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@heimlershistory) — THE APUSH video resource. Covers every period, every key concept. Also has AP World and AP Gov content. Free.
- **Adam Norris** — [apushreview.com](https://www.apushreview.com/) — fill-in-the-blank review sheets for every period, final exam review videos, MCQ & SAQ practice. All free. Period review sheets link to specific videos.

### Study Sites
- **Gilder Lehrman AP US History Study Guide** — [gilderlehrman.org/ap-us-history](https://gilderlehrman.org/ap-us-history) — primary sources, essays, videos organized by time period. Practice MCQs. Free.
- **Fiveable APUSH** — unit guides, DBQ/LEQ/SAQ guides.

### Exam Format
- 55 MCQ (55 min) — 40% of score (source-based)
- 3 SAQ (40 min) — 20% of score
- 1 DBQ (60 min, includes 15 min reading) — 25% of score
- 1 LEQ (40 min) — 15% of score

### Key Strategy
APUSH is about ARGUMENT, not memorization. The DBQ and LEQ reward students who can make a defensible claim and support it with evidence. Practice writing thesis statements and using evidence — don't just memorize facts.

## AP Precalculus

### Official (College Board)
- **AP Precalculus CED** — [PDF on AP Central](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap-precalculus-course-and-exam-description.pdf)
- **AP Classroom** — Progress Checks, AP Daily videos
- **2025 Chief Reader Report** — [PDF](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap25-cr-report-precalculus.pdf) — shows exactly where students lost points on FRQs. Read this.

### Video Resources
- **Khan Academy** — search for precalculus topics. Covers all function types in the AP curriculum.
- **AP Daily videos** — in AP Classroom, short videos by AP teachers on every topic.

### Study Sites
- **Fiveable AP Precalc** — [fiveable.me/ap-pre-calc](https://fiveable.me/ap-pre-calc) — unit study guides, cheatsheets, FRQ practice, score calculator.
- **FreeTestPrep AP Precalculus** — [freetestprep.com](https://freetestprep.com/ap-precalculus-study-guide-unit-by-unit-plan-with-free-practice-drills/) — unit-by-unit plan with free practice drills.

### Unit Weighting
| Unit | MCQ Weight | FRQ |
|------|-----------|-----|
| Unit 1: Polynomial & Rational Functions | 30-40% | FRQ 3 (no calculator) |
| Unit 2: Exponential & Logarithmic Functions | 27-40% | FRQ 1 (calculator), FRQ 4 (no calculator) |
| Unit 3: Trigonometric & Polar Functions | 30-35% | FRQ 2 (calculator) |
| Unit 4: Parameters, Vectors, Matrices | 5-7% (MCQ only) | Not on FRQ |

**Focus:** Units 1-3 are the tested units. Unit 4 is MCQ only and low weight. Master the first three.

### Chief Reader Insights (2025)
- **Limit notation is crucial** — students struggle with it across multiple topics (1.6, 1.7, 1.9, 1.10, 2.3, 2.11). Practice it early.
- **Function composition and inverses** (Topics 2.7, 2.8) — weak spot. Practice these before inverse trig (3.9).
- **FRQ 4 is no-calculator symbolic manipulation** — Units 2 and 3. Algebraic fluency matters.

### Exam Format
- 40 MCQ (120 min) — 62.5% of score
  - 29 no-calculator questions
  - 12 calculator-active questions (actually ~11)
- 4 FRQ (60 min) — 37.5% of score
  - FRQ 1 & 2: calculator required
  - FRQ 3 & 4: no calculator
- 3 hours total

## AP English Language & Composition

### Official (College Board)
- **AP Lang CED** — on AP Central
- **AP Classroom** — Progress Checks, AP Daily videos
- **Past FRQs + Scoring Guidelines + Student Samples** — every year on AP Central. The student samples with scorer commentary are incredibly useful — you see what a 6 looks like vs a 3.

### Study Sites
- **Fiveable AP Lang** — [fiveable.me/ap-lang](https://fiveable.me/ap-lang) — rhetorical analysis guides, synthesis guides, argument guides. Breakdown of the 6-point rubric for each FRQ.
- **PrepScholar AP Lang Practice** — [blog.prepscholar.com](https://blog.prepscholar.com/ap-english-language-and-composition-practice-exams) — lists every available practice exam and rates their quality.

### Note on Khan Academy
Khan Academy does NOT have a dedicated AP Lang course. Use College Board materials + Fiveable + practice exams instead.

### Exam Format
- 45 MCQ (60 min) — 45% of score
  - 23-25 reading questions (rhetorical analysis of passages)
  - 20-22 writing questions (revision of texts)
- 3 FRQ (135 min) — 55% of score
  - FRQ 1: Synthesis Essay (15 min reading + 40 min writing) — use 3+ sources to build an argument
  - FRQ 2: Rhetorical Analysis (40 min) — analyze rhetorical choices in a passage
  - FRQ 3: Argument (40 min) — build an argument on a given topic

### Key Strategy (from 2025 Chief Reader)
- **Rhetorical Analysis (FRQ 2):** Mean score was 3.30/6. Most students summarize instead of analyze. The rubric rewards COMMENTARY — explaining WHY a choice works, not just naming it. "The author uses metaphor" = summary. "The author uses metaphor to make the abstract concept of X tangible, making the reader feel Y" = analysis.
- **Synthesis (FRQ 1):** Use at least 3 sources. Don't just quote them — explain how they support your argument.
- **Argument (FRQ 3):** Need a defensible thesis + specific evidence. Vague examples lose points.

### 2025 FRQ 2 Mean: 3.30/6
This means most students get half points. If you can write genuine analysis (not summary), you're already above average.

## How to Use These Resources

### Daily (5-10 min)
- AP Classroom AP Daily video for whatever unit you're in
- Anki cards from that day's material

### Weekly (30-60 min per AP class)
- Watch 1-2 topic videos (Heimler, Bozeman, OCT, Flipping Physics — depending on class)
- Do the Progress Check for the current unit in AP Classroom
- Review past FRQs for the current unit — read the prompt, attempt it, then read the scoring guidelines

### Before Tests
- Do past FRQs from that unit — time yourself
- Review the Chief Reader Report for common mistakes
- Use Fiveable cheatsheets for quick review

### Before AP Exams (April)
- Full practice exams (released exams on AP Central)
- Review all FRQs from past 3 years
- Watch the "cram review" videos (5-hour reviews exist for most subjects)
