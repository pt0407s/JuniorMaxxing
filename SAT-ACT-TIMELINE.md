# SAT / ACT Timeline — Junior Year 2026-27

> You're aiming for 100s in 6 APs. The SAT/ACT can't be ignored, but it also
> can't be allowed to cannibalize AP prep. This is the strategy to avoid that
> collision.

## The core problem

AP exams are May 3–12, 2027. SAT/ACT prep that overlaps with AP exam prep
(April–May) will hurt both. **Take your SAT/ACT BEFORE April 2027.**

## Recommended test plan

### Option A: SAT-first (recommended for most)

| Attempt | Test Date | Registration Deadline | Why this date |
|---------|-----------|----------------------|---------------|
| 1st | **Nov 7, 2026** | Oct 23, 2026 | Early baseline. Fall junior year. Low pressure. |
| 2nd | **Mar 6, 2027** | Feb 19, 2027 | After 4 months of prep. 2 months before AP exams. |
| 3rd (if needed) | **June 5, 2027** | May 21, 2027 | After AP exams. Last shot before senior year apps. |

### Option B: ACT-first (if your practice scores are higher on ACT)

| Attempt | Test Date | Registration Deadline | Why this date |
|---------|-----------|----------------------|---------------|
| 1st | **Dec 12, 2026** | Nov 6, 2026 | Early baseline. After fall semester is rolling. |
| 2nd | **Feb 27, 2027** | Jan 22, 2027 | After focused prep. 2 months before AP exams. |
| 3rd (if needed) | **June 12, 2027** | May 7, 2027 | After AP exams. |

### What NOT to do

- **Do NOT take April 10 ACT** — 3 weeks before AP exams. You'll split focus.
- **Do NOT take May 1 SAT** — 2 days before AP Biology. Disaster.
- **Do NOT prep SAT/ACT during April 2027** — that's AP crunch time.
- **Do NOT take both SAT and ACT** — pick one, commit, master it.

## How to decide SAT vs ACT

Before Sept 12, 2026 (SAT) or Sept 19, 2026 (ACT):

1. Take a free SAT practice test on Khan Academy (digital, ~2.5 hrs)
2. Take a free ACT practice test on act.org (~3 hrs)
3. Compare scores using an SAT/ACT concordance table
4. Pick the one you scored higher on relative to the percentile
5. Commit. Don't second-guess.

**Quick heuristic:** Strong reader, good at algebra, hates science section → SAT.
Fast reader, good at science reasoning, likes fast pace → ACT.

## Budgeting time alongside AP classes

The key insight: **SAT/ACT prep is 2-3 hours/week. AP classes are 3-4 hours/day.**
The SAT is a smaller time commitment than people think. The danger isn't the
time — it's the mental switching cost. Don't study SAT on the same day you have
an AP test. Don't study SAT the week before a major AP unit test.

**Rule:** SAT prep happens on weekends or light homework nights. Never the night
before an AP assessment.

## Free resources

- **SAT:** Khan Academy Official SAT Prep (free, personalized, from College Board)
- **ACT:** ACT Academy (free)
- **Both:** CrackSAT.net, PrepScholar blog (free strategy guides)

Don't pay for a prep course unless you take the diagnostic, prep for 2 months
on free resources, and still aren't improving. Most students don't need paid
prep.
