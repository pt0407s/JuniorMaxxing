## Critical observation for 100s

The 1st and 4th 6-week periods are the SHORTEST (25 and 24 days). That means:
- Fewer assignments to average → each one weighs more
- One bad test = harder to recover
- **Front-load perfection in these periods** — bank your 100 early
- The longer periods (3rd, 6th = 29-32 days) give more room to absorb a slip
