## Unit breakdown (CED)

| Unit | Topic | MCQ Weight | FRQ | Priority |
|------|-------|-----------|-----|----------|
| 1 | Polynomial & Rational Functions | 30-40% | FRQ 3 (no calc) | **HIGH** |
| 2 | Exponential & Logarithmic Functions | 27-40% | FRQ 1 (calc), FRQ 4 (no calc) | **HIGH** |
| 3 | Trigonometric & Polar Functions | 30-35% | FRQ 2 (calc) | **HIGH** |
| 4 | Parameters, Vectors, Matrices | 5-7% (MCQ only) | Not on FRQ | Low |

**Focus:** Units 1-3 are the tested units. Unit 4 is MCQ only and low weight. Master the first three.

### Chief Reader Insights (2025):
- **Limit notation is crucial** — students struggle across multiple topics (1.6, 1.7, 1.9, 1.10, 2.3, 2.11). Practice it early.
- **Function composition and inverses** (Topics 2.7, 2.8) — weak spot. Practice before inverse trig (3.9).
- **FRQ 4 is no-calculator symbolic manipulation** — Units 2 and 3. Algebraic fluency matters.

## Test prep protocol (starts 5 days before test)

See [test-prep-checklist.md](../test-prep-checklist.md) for the full checklist.

### Precalc-specific test prep:
- **Day -5:** Make a 1-page formula sheet + when to use each
- **Day -4:** Do a mixed problem set (interleaved — mix polynomial + exponential + trig)
- **Day -3:** Do past FRQs — practice both calculator and no-calculator sections
- **Day -2:** Review error log. Drill trig identities until automatic. Practice limit notation.
- **Day -1:** One more mixed problem set. Check all work by hand (not Desmos). Sleep 8 hours.

### On the test:
- **Show all work** — teachers give partial credit. Wrong answer + good work > right answer + no work
- **Use Desmos to check** but never to replace hand-work (if calculator allowed)
- **Trig identities** — if you can't remember one, derive it from sin²+cos²=1
- **Check units and reasonableness** — does the answer make sense?

## 6-week approach
- Math tests are the most predictable — if you can do the homework problems, you can do the test
- **Do homework the day it's assigned** while the lesson is fresh
- **Before tests:** Mixed problem set (interleaved) from all sections covered, not just re-doing homework
- **Error log review** the night before every test — this is the highest-ROI habit for math
- **Interleaved practice** — DON'T do 20 of the same problem type. Mix types within a session (d=0.83 effect size, Rohrer 2020)
