## Anki cards to make

| Type | Examples | Est. cards/day |
|------|----------|----------------|
| Formulas + WHEN to use | v=v₀+at, F=ma, W=Fd, KE=½mv² | 2-3 |
| Key definitions | Inertia, net force, work, power | 1-2 |
| Unit conversions | km→m, g→kg, mph→m/s | 1 |
| Assumptions | "When to use conservation of energy vs momentum" | 1-2 |

**Total: ~5-8 new cards/day**

**Critical:** Don't just memorize formulas. Card the CONDITIONS — when does each formula apply? What are the assumptions? The equation sheet is given on the exam; knowing WHICH to use is the test.

## Test prep protocol (starts 5 days before test)

See [test-prep-checklist.md](../test-prep-checklist.md) for the full checklist.

### Physics-specific test prep:
- **Day -5:** Make a 1-page summary: every formula + when to use + assumptions
- **Day -4:** Do a mixed problem set (interleaved — mix kinematics + forces + energy + momentum)
- **Day -3:** Do past FRQs — practice pacing, FRQs are long and multipart
- **Day -2:** Review error log. Practice free-body diagrams and bar charts (LOL diagrams)
- **Day -1:** One more mixed problem set. Sleep 8 hours.

### On the test:
- **Draw free-body diagrams for EVERY force problem** — teachers grade for them
- **Check units on every answer** — dimensional analysis catches 80% of mistakes
- **Show every step in pencil** — partial credit is huge in physics
- **If you can't solve it:** write the relevant equations and what you'd do — partial credit
- **FRQs are multipart:** if you're stuck on part (b), write something for (c) using variables
- Skip and return if stuck on a hard part

## 6-week approach
- Physics tests reward problem-solving over memorization
- **Before tests:** Mixed problem set (interleaved) — never do all kinematics then all forces
- **FRQs are long and multipart** — practice pacing. Skip and return if stuck.
- **Master energy bar charts (LOL diagrams) and momentum bar charts** — they make 60% of problems easy
- **Most concept-heavy science AP** — math is easy, reasoning is hard. Focus on understanding WHY, not just HOW.
