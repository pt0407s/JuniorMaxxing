# Grading — All Classes

Every class at Legacy Ranch HS uses the same grading split:

| Category | Weight | Includes |
|----------|--------|----------|
| Major | 60% | Tests, Exams, Projects, Labs |
| Minor | 40% | Daily classwork, homework, quizzes |

## The 97+ math (same for every class)

- `0.6(Major) + 0.4(Minor) = Overall`
- To hit 97: Major ≥ 95, Minor = 100 → `0.6(95) + 0.4(100) = 97.0`
- To hit 100: Major = 100, Minor = 100 → `0.6(100) + 0.4(100) = 100.0`
- If you slip on a test (85): `0.6(85) + 0.4(100) = 91.0` — that's why Minor can't save a bad Major

## The strategy (same for every class)

1. **Minor (40%) = your insurance.** Never miss homework, classwork, or quiz prep. Aim for 100 in this category every 6 weeks. This is fully in your control.
2. **Major (60%) = where it's won or lost.** Tests, labs, projects. One bad test drops you ~1.2 points overall. Can't afford more than 1-2 slips per 6-week period.
3. **In short 6-week periods (1st = 25 days, 4th = 24 days), there are fewer assignments.** Each one weighs more. Front-load perfection.
