# Grading — All Classes

Every class at Legacy Ranch HS uses the same grading split:

| Category | Weight | Includes |
|----------|--------|----------|
| Major | 60% | Tests, Exams, Projects, Labs |
| Minor | 40% | Daily classwork, homework, quizzes |

## The 100 math (same for every class)

- `0.6(Major) + 0.4(Minor) = Overall`
- To hit 100: **Major = 100 AND Minor = 100** — no room for error
- A single 98 on a test: `0.6(98) + 0.4(100) = 98.8` — you're already below 100
- A single 95 on a test: `0.6(95) + 0.4(100) = 97.0` — 100 is gone for that 6-week period

## The brutal reality of targeting 100

100 requires **perfection in both categories.** This is different from 97:
- At 97, you can absorb a 95 on a test if your Minor is 100
- At 100, you cannot absorb ANYTHING. Every test, every lab, every homework, every quiz = 100

### What this actually requires:
1. **Minor (40%): 100 on every single assignment.** Never miss homework, never bomb a quiz, never skip classwork. This is the "easy" part — it's in your control.
2. **Major (60%): 100 on every test, lab, and project.** This is the hard part. It means:
   - No careless mistakes on tests
   - Perfect lab reports every time
   - No points lost to formatting, units, or missing sections
   - Knowing the material cold before walking in

### The margin for error is ZERO
- One homework zero = Minor drops = 100 is gone
- One test with a dumb mistake = Major drops = 100 is gone
- One lab with missing error analysis = Major drops = 100 is gone

### How to make this achievable
1. **Never lose Minor points** — this is 40% and fully controllable. Do every assignment, prep every quiz, participate every class.
2. **Over-prepare for every Major** — the 3-day test prep protocol in STRATEGY.md isn't optional, it's mandatory. You need to walk into every test knowing you'll get 100.
3. **Lab reports: use the rubric as a checklist.** Every section, every point. Don't leave anything on the table.
4. **Check your work on tests.** If you finish early, re-check every answer. Most lost points are careless mistakes, not knowledge gaps.
5. **Go to the teacher after every test** — even if you got 100. Build the relationship. If you ever do slip, you want the teacher to know you care and offer a way to recover.
6. **In short 6-week periods (1st = 25 days, 4th = 24 days), there are fewer assignments.** Each one weighs more. One slip = 100 is gone. Front-load perfection.
