## Block 5: Write your if-then plans (15 min)

Get a physical piece of paper. Write these down. Literally. On paper. Put it on your desk.

- **IF** I sit down to study and want to check my phone, **THEN** I will put my phone in another room and do one Anki card.
- **IF** I get home from school, **THEN** before doing anything else, I will do one Anki card.
- **IF** I feel too tired to study, **THEN** I will set a 15-minute timer, lie down, then do one practice problem.
- **IF** I miss a day, **THEN** I will not feel guilty. I will do one card the next day. Never miss twice.

## What NOT to do on Tuesday

Don't try to do everything. You can't pre-study 6 APs in one day. Don't try.

Don't:
- Try to read every document in the repo
- Make Anki cards for all 6 classes
- Watch 6 hours of AP videos
- Stay up late "preparing" — sleep matters more than prep
- Stress about what you haven't done. You have the systems. You have the plans. You just need to execute one day at a time.
